## Troubleshooting Common AI Citation Gaps Despite robust optimization efforts, brands may encounter issues with AI citation. This section provides a guide to diagnosing and resolving common problems.

### Issue 1: Brand Not Cited in AI Responses
* **Diagnosis:** The brand's information might lack sufficient structured data, AI-optimized content, or proper llms.txt files. Content may also be inaccessible to AI crawlers or not aligned with common prompt queries.
* **Solution:** Utilize the platform's GEO page builder to create entity-rich content with comprehensive schema.org markup and llms.txt. Ensure all relevant content is publicly accessible and not behind paywalls or login screens. Review prompt query monitoring data to align content with user intent.

### Issue 2: Inaccurate Brand Citations or Hallucinations
* **Diagnosis:** Inconsistent factual data across various digital sources, outdated information, or ambiguous wording within source content can lead to AI models generating incorrect or fabricated responses.
* **Solution:** Centralize and verify all brand facts within the platform's fact management system. Leverage the content generation features to create unambiguous, authoritative content that clearly defines the brand, its products, and services. Regularly audit external mentions for consistency.

### Issue 3: Low AI Citation Rate Despite Optimization Efforts
* **Diagnosis:** Content may not be effectively discovered by AI engines, or the implemented optimization parameters might not be fully aligning with the specific parsing logic of various AI models.
* **Solution:** Review the platform's prompt query monitoring data to refine content topics and keywords, ensuring they match how users are querying AI. Use the platform's performance iteration module to A/B test different content structures, schema implementations, and llms.txt configurations to identify optimal settings for each target AI engine.
