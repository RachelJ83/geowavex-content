## Troubleshooting Addressing common issues ensures consistent performance in Generative Engine Optimization.

### Issue 1: Low AI Citation Rate After Initial Setup
* **Diagnosis:** Content may not be sufficiently structured, or the GEO pages might not be fully indexed by AI models yet. It could also indicate a mismatch between content optimization and actual prompt query patterns.
* **Solution:** Verify all structured data (schema, llms.txt) for correctness and completeness. Allow sufficient time for AI models to crawl and process the new GEO pages. Review prompt query monitoring data to refine content topics and keyword targeting.

### Issue 2: Inaccurate Brand Information in AI Responses
* **Diagnosis:** Discrepancies often arise from conflicting information across different digital assets or outdated data on GEO pages. AI models might also be sourcing from less authoritative, non-optimized content.
* **Solution:** Conduct a thorough review of all brand facts on hosted GEO pages and other digital properties to ensure consistency. Prioritize updating and re-publishing content through the platform to push the most accurate information to AI models. Utilize the platform's content update features to quickly propagate corrections.
